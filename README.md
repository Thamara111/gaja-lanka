# Gaja Lanka Website Package

A comprehensive multi-page website and empirical data repository dedicated to the Sri Lankan Elephant (*Elephas maximus maximus*).

## Pages & Structure:
- `index.html`: Master Home portal with Hero section, key metrics, chapter gateways, myths vs. facts, and dataset download links.
- `overview.html`: Species Profile & Taxonomy — biological traits, depigmentation, rare tusks, and keystone ecology.
- `history.html`: History & Cultural Heritage — 2,500 years of reverence, royal chronicles, Esala Perahera, and legendary tuskers.
- `massacre.html`: British Colonial Massacre — 19th-century bounty hunting, Major Thomas Rogers, and highland extirpation.
- `habitats.html`: Habitats & Migration — The Great Gathering at Minneriya, national park explorer tabs, and migratory corridors.
- `wild-domestic.html`: Wild vs. Domestic Elephants — captive care, Pinnawala Orphanage, ETH Udawalawe rewilding, and welfare laws.
- `statistics.html`: Data & Trends — interactive charts (population from 1800–2025, annual casualties, causes of death) and full data tables.
- `hec.html`: Human-Elephant Conflict — root causes, retaliatory weapons (Hakka Patas, illegal electrocution), and seasonal fencing solutions.
- `news.html`: Live News Wire — real-time Google News RSS API integration and verified field dispatches.

## Datasets:
- `data.json`: Population milestones, annual HEC casualty records, mortality causes, and news API endpoint.
- `elephant_population_and_mortality_stats.csv`: Full statistical time-series table from 1800 to 2025.

## Assets:
- `assets/css/styles.css`: Central stylesheet for glassmorphism, custom scrollbars, and active state styles.
- `assets/js/main.js`: Global navigation, active page highlighting, mobile menu, and back-to-top button.
- `assets/js/habitats.js`: Interactive national park dossier tab switcher.
- `assets/js/statistics.js`: Chart.js configuration and rendering logic for interactive statistical graphs.
- `assets/js/news.js`: Live RSS news wire fetch and topic filtering.
- `assets/images/`: Extracted high-resolution photographic imagery.
