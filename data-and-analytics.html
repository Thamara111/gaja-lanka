Drivers of Elephant Mortality

Over 84% of documented elephant fatalities are direct outcomes of rural land skirmishes, illegal improvised perimeter explosives, and unsanctioned farm-line electrification.

Hakka Patas (Improvised Explosive Baits)
167 incidents 38%

Crude gunpowder & detonator concealed in pumpkin or melon. Fatal oral cavity trauma.

Illegal Grid Electrocution (Agricultural Taps)
105 incidents 24%

High-voltage tap-ins directly from national domestic lines bypassing safety trip relays.

Gunshot Wounds (Crop Protection Fire)
70 incidents 16%

Trap-guns or muzzle-loaders causing septicemia, osteomyelitis, and gradual starvation.

Railway Corridor Collisions
40 incidents 9%

Concentrated in Batticaloa, Trincomalee, and Talaimannar night-mail segments.

Natural Causes / Advanced Age / Undetermined
57 incidents 13%

Molar senescence, drought dehydration, inter-bull combat, and advanced decomposing carcasses.

Epistemic Governance & Research Ethics

Scientific Caveats, Methodology & Citation Standards

history_edu

1. Census Discontinuity

Waterhole counts rely upon synchronized dry-season observation of watering points over 48 hours. This methodology intentionally omits dense wet-zone montane rainforest populations (Sinharaja, Peak Wilderness) where perennial streams prevent waterhole aggregation.

DWC Baseline Audit (2011) § 4.2
timelapse

2. Necropsy Verification Lag

Mortality figures represent recovered and officially inspected carcasses. Carcasses deep inside state forest reserves that undergo natural rapid decay before discovery are under-reported. Laboratory toxicological confirmations often trail field recovery by 60–90 days.

Veterinary Forensics Directive 08/2023
menu_book

3. Standard Citation Rule

Publications utilizing these records must cite: "Gaja Lanka Collaborative Spatial Repository (2026), Department of Wildlife Conservation (Sri Lanka) and Center for Conservation and Research (CCR) joint data synthesis, Version 2026.1."

DOI: 10.1016/j.biocon.2026.108920
