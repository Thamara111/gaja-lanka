DECEASED • RECENT TRAGEDY (OCT 2026) Wild Specimen
11 ft Tallest Wild Giant

Kawanthissa

කාවන්තිස්ස (ගල්ගමුවේ ඇතා)

Regarded by wildlife biologists, photographers, and rural communities as the tallest and most magnificent wild tusker roaming the island in modern decades. Kawanthissa possessed extraordinary physical stature, standing nearly 11 feet tall with heavy, outward-swept ivory tusks. He represented the pinnacle of dry-zone pachyderm genetics in the northwestern province.

LIVING WILD GIANT • VETERINARY RECOVERY Wild Specimen
Anuradhapura Wilds

Agbo of Thirappane

තිරප්පනේ අග්බෝ

Sri Lanka's most closely monitored living wild tusker. Agbo is a legendary solitary bull elephant of the ancient tank cascades of Anuradhapura who became a national hero in 2023 when he survived life-threatening gunshot injuries through an unprecedented multi-month veterinary rescue operation.

LIVING WILD GIANT • MONARCH OF THE GATHERING Wild Specimen
Minneriya Gathering

Mahasen of Minneriya

මින්නේරියේ මහසෙන් ඇතා

Named after the revered ancient monarch King Mahasen (who engineered the Great Minneriya Tank in 276–303 CE), this imposing wild patriarch is the crowning jewel of the annual Minneriya Elephant Gathering.

DECEASED WILD MARTYR • CROSSED TUSKS Wild Specimen
Crossed Ivory Legend

Galgamuwa Dala Poottuwa

ගල්ගමුවේ දළ පූට්ටුවා

A mythic wild tusker celebrated for his extraordinarily rare 'Dala Poottuwa' morphology—tusks that crossed over each other symmetrically in front of his trunk. His brutal slaying by poachers in 2017 became the turning point in Sri Lankan wildlife law enforcement.

DECEASED • NATIONAL TREASURE (10.5 FT) Sacred Sovereign
10.5 ft Tallest in Asia

Nadungamuwa Vijaya Raja

නැදුන්ගමුවේ විජය රාජා

Asia's tallest tamed tusker of the 21st century and an undisputed national hero. Declared a National Cultural Treasure by Presidential decree, Nadungamuwa Raja served as the Chief Relic Bearer of the Sacred Tooth Relic casket for 15 consecutive seasons.

DECEASED • 50-YEAR SACRED SOVEREIGN Sacred Sovereign
50-Year Relic Bearer

Maligawa Raja

මාලිගාවේ රාජා

The most famous sacred elephant in the modern history of Buddhism. Maligawa Raja served as the Chief Relic Bearer of the Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic for an astounding 50 years without a single behavioral infraction.

LIVING • REIGNING CHIEF RELIC BEARER Sacred Sovereign
Gift of Rajiv Gandhi

Indi Raja

ඉන්දි රාජා

The reigning supreme temple tusker of Sri Lanka. Indi Raja carries the golden Karanduwa in the Kandy Esala Perahera today, inheriting the mantle of Maligawa Raja and Nadungamuwa Raja.

DECEASED • LONGEST TUSKS IN ASIA Sacred Sovereign
7.5 ft Longest Tusks in Asia

Millangoda Raja

මිල්ලන්ගොඩ රාජා

Famous throughout the zoological world for possessing the longest tusks recorded on any living Asian elephant in the 20th and 21st centuries, measuring an incredible 7.5 feet each.

LIVING • RUHUNU DEITY TUSKER Sacred Sovereign
Ruhunu Chief Tusker

Wasana of Kataragama

කතරගම වාසනා

The beloved chief sacred tusker dedicated to the shrine of God Skanda-Murugan in the holy city of Kataragama.

DECEASED • NATIONAL MUSEUM MONUMENT Sacred Sovereign
Kelaniya & Kandy Bearer

Heiyantuduwa Raja

හෙයියන්තුඩුවේ රාජා

A revered temple tusker whose owner took the visionary step of donating his entire skeleton to science, where it stands today as the premier zoological exhibit in the National Museum of Colombo.
